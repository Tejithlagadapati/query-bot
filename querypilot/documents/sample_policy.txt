Expenses can be claimed for the following categories:
- Software: For purchasing, renewing, or upgrading software licenses and subscriptions.
- Cloud: For cloud computing services such as AWS, Azure, GCP, and related infrastructure costs.
- Travel: For costs associated with business travel including flights, hotels, and ground transportation.

Policy Guidelines:
- All expenses must be reasonable and necessary for business purposes.
- Each expense should be accurately categorized to ensure proper tracking.
- Large expenses may require additional documentation or approvals.
