EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version 1.0

1. GENERAL BUSINESS EXPENSES

Employees may claim reimbursement for business expenses that are necessary for their work and supported by a valid receipt or invoice. Personal expenses are not reimbursable. Claims without supporting documents will be returned to the employee for correction.

2. SOFTWARE EXPENSES

Software subscriptions and licences are reimbursable when they are needed for a business purpose. Any single software expense above ₹10,000 requires approval from the employee's manager before the purchase is made. Software expenses of ₹10,000 or less do not require manager approval but must still be recorded with a description of their purpose.

3. CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE EXPENSES

All cloud infrastructure expenses must include a project reference when they are submitted. Cloud expenses without a project reference will not be processed until the reference is provided. Departments are responsible for monitoring their cloud spending against the budget allocated to them.

4. TRAVEL EXPENSES

Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted after this deadline may be rejected unless a manager provides a written explanation for the delay. Travel claims must include tickets, accommodation receipts and a short description of the purpose of the trip.

5. SUBMISSION AND REVIEW

Expense claims are reviewed by the finance team within 10 working days of submission. Employees will be notified if a claim is approved, rejected or needs more information.
