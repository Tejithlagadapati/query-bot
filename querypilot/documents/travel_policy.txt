TRAVEL POLICY

1. BOOKING
Employees should book flights and hotels through the approved travel desk. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours.

2. APPROVAL
Any trip with total estimated costs above ₹50,000 needs approval from the department head before booking. Trips below this limit need manager approval only.

3. DAILY ALLOWANCE
Employees receive a daily meal allowance of ₹1,500 on approved trips. Receipts are not required for the allowance.
