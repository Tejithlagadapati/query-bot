CLOUD SPENDING POLICY

1. PURPOSE
This policy controls spending on cloud hosting, storage and compute resources.

2. PROJECT TAGGING
Every cloud resource must be tagged with a project code. Untagged resources may be shut down after 7 days.

3. BUDGET ALERTS
Finance will notify a department when its cloud spending reaches 80 percent of its budget. Spending above 100 percent of budget needs approval from the department head.
